### SDSC Summer Institute 2026
# Session 5.2 Performance Tuning

**Date:** Thursday, August 6, 2026

**Time:** 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM Pacific

**Summary:** This session provides an introduction to the topic of how to best use computers to get to the desired result fast. The major objective of the material is to teach you how to improve performance of software codes, but you can only do that if you understand what slows compute down. The content thus emphasizes understanding of the compute environment much more than providing ready-to-use recipes.

**Presented by:** [Igor Sfiligoi](https://www.sdsc.edu/research/experts/sfiligoi_igor.html)
